How It Works

1. Post Clear Details

Create a lost or found report with a good title, location, date, and item description. Photos can improve match quality and reduce confusion.

2. Search Smartly

Use filters by type, category, and status to quickly find similar reports. This helps narrow down potential matches in a few clicks.

3. Recover And Close

Once your item is returned, mark the report as recovered. Keeping statuses updated helps others focus on unresolved items.

Why Use This Platform

Campus Lost & Found creates a consistent process for everyone. Students can post quickly, staff can verify details, and finders can report responsibly. The goal is simple: fewer lost items left unclaimed and a smoother recovery process for the whole campus community.

Accurate reports save time for everyone. Include distinguishing details like color, brand, unique marks, and where you last saw the item.

Reporting Tips For Faster Matches

  • Use a specific title, for example "Black Lenovo Laptop in Sleeve" instead of "Lost Laptop".
  • Describe the last known location as precisely as possible, such as building and floor.
  • Add one or two unique identifiers that only the owner would know.
  • Keep your contact information up to date so replies are not delayed.
  • Mark recovered reports quickly to keep search results clean.
